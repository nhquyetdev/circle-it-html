How to play

  • Hold & drag to draw. Close a loop around a boss to hit it.
  • Tighter loop = more damage: PERFECT ×1.5, GOOD ×1, SLOPPY ×0.6.
  • One loop around several bosses = combo bonus.
  • If a boss or its attacks touch your line, you take damage. Lift your finger to make the line vanish.
  • Your pen follows your finger at a limited speed — AGI makes it faster.
  • Skills: 1 Freeze · 2 Ink Shield · 3 Power Ring (or tap the buttons).
  • Each stage has a 3 minute limit. Beat 10 bosses, then survive multi-boss stages.
